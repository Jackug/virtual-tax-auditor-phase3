Stage 3 — Data Relationships & Preparation

Define how the selected datasets relate before detection logic is applied. The engine checks join keys, missing identities, unmatched taxpayers and many-to-many multiplication.

For a join such as Income Tax to VAT, define the identity relationship explicitly (for example TIN = TIN) and add Financial Year or Month as an additional relationship where required.
