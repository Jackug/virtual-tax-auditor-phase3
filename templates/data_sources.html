Step 2C Control Principles

2C-1 Profile Measure structure, completeness, types and quality.
2C-2 Classify Separate critical issues from reviewable exceptions.
2C-3 Propose Generate controlled cleaning without changing the original.
2C-4 Resolve Human decision is recorded for each exception.
2C-5 Version Cleaning creates a traceable derivative.
2C-6 Approve Only approved data is eligible for VTA use.
